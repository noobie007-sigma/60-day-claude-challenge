# 🦈 AI Shark Tank Simulator

An interactive **AI Shark Tank Simulator** built as a **single self-contained HTML file** using **HTML, CSS, and JavaScript**.

No backend. No frameworks. Just open `index.html` in your browser and start pitching your startup.

---

# 🚀 Features

## Startup Pitch Form

Users can enter:

* Startup Name
* Problem Statement
* Solution
* Revenue Model
* Target Audience
* Funding Ask

---

## AI Judges

Four AI judges evaluate every startup.

### 🦈 Venture Capitalist

Focus:

* Market Size
* Scalability

### 🦈 Founder

Focus:

* Execution
* Team

### 🦈 Customer

Focus:

* Product usefulness
* Customer value

### 🦈 Angel Investor

Focus:

* Profitability
* Financial sustainability

---

## Interactive Pitch Round

Each judge asks two questions.

Users submit answers and receive dynamic AI reactions.

---

## AI Scoring

The startup is scored out of **100** across five categories.

* Market Potential
* Innovation
* Business Model
* Execution
* Investment Worthiness

---

## Final Investment Decision

Possible outcomes:

* ✅ Invest
* ❌ Reject
* 🏢 Acquire
* ⏳ Come Back Later

The simulator also generates:

* Suggested Valuation
* Recommended Funding Amount
* Investment Reasoning
