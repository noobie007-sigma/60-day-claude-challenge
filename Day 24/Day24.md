# Executive Summary

AI CareerPrep Platform is an AI-powered career preparation platform designed specifically for Indian engineering students and early-career software engineers.

The platform helps users prepare for placements through:

- Personalized AI Roadmaps
- ATS Resume Analysis
- Adaptive Interview Preparation
- DSA Practice Guidance
- Career Growth Tracking
- Placement Readiness Assessment

The startup addresses a large and growing EdTech market while solving a highly painful problem: students struggle to find structured, personalized guidance during placement preparation.

The opportunity is significant, but commercial validation remains unproven.

**Overall Investment Score:** 64/100

**Verdict:** 🟡 VALIDATE
