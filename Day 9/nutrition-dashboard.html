📚 Sources & Assumptions

Guidelines Used

Primary: ICMR-NIN 2020 — Nutrient Requirements for Indians (Indian Council of Medical Research, National Institute of Nutrition). Used for energy, protein, and micronutrient RDAs.

Fallback for nutrients not in ICMR-NIN: WHO/FAO 2004 Human Vitamin and Mineral Requirements; US DRI (Institute of Medicine) for vitamin D, B12.

Energy equation: Mifflin-St Jeor (1990) BMR × PAL activity factor. Muscle gain adjustment: +350 kcal/day.

Food Composition Data Sources

ICMR-NIN Nutritive Value of Indian Foods IFCT 2017 (Indian Food Composition Tables) USDA FoodData Central (global foods) Manufacturer estimates (packaged foods)

⚠️ Key Assumptions & Limitations

  • All nutrient values are approximate estimates — actual values vary by cooking method, variety, ripeness, and brand.
  • Portion sizes estimated from user descriptions using standard household measures.
  • Bioavailability not modelled (e.g. non-haem iron absorption ~10–15% vs haem iron ~25%).
  • Cooking losses not consistently applied across all nutrients.
  • Supplement intake not included.
  • Targets are population RDAs, not individualised clinical recommendations.
  • This tool does not replace clinical nutrition assessment.
