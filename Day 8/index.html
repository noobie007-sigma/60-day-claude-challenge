🚨 Biggest Anomaly

Ghaziabad vs Bangalore gap is 108 AQI points — despite both being major Indian urban centers, Ghaziabad's AQI of 152 is 3.45× worse than Bangalore's 44. This extraordinary gap is driven by Ghaziabad's proximity to Delhi's industrial belt, dense traffic corridors, construction dust from rapid urbanization, and the lack of green buffer zones.

😲 Most Surprising Observation

Delhi's AQI (73) is currently LOWER than Kolkata's (103) — a rare reversal of the usual narrative. Delhi has historically been India's most polluted capital, but June 2026 data shows pre-monsoon wind patterns and recent policy measures (odd-even vehicle rules, industrial shutdowns) have temporarily pushed Kolkata above Delhi. Meanwhile, Ghaziabad — just 20km from Delhi — remains significantly more polluted, confirming that the NCR outer belt still suffers from worse air than the capital itself.

📋 Executive Summary

Analysis of 10 major Indian cities (June 2026) reveals a sharp north-south air quality divide. Northern cities — Ghaziabad (152), Delhi (73), Noida (79), and Kolkata (103) — dominate the pollution rankings, driven by vehicular emissions, industrial activity, and dust. Southern and western coastal cities — Bangalore (44), Mumbai (56), and Pune (59) — enjoy significantly cleaner air, benefiting from sea breezes, monsoon proximity, and greener urban planning.


The average AQI of 79 across all cities falls in the "Moderate" band (Indian CPCB scale), though 4 of 10 cities exceed this average. India's PM2.5 concentration of 48.9 µg/m³ in 2025 is nearly 10× the WHO guideline, placing the country 6th globally in air pollution. Water quality mirrors air quality disparities — northern cities show very high TDS and hardness levels, adversely impacting skin and hair health, while Mumbai and Bangalore supply treated water of superior quality.


For Ghaziabad residents: environmental health risks are among India's highest. Immediate action is recommended: N95 masks outdoors, HEPA air purifiers indoors, RO water filters, and regular health check-ups for respiratory and cardiovascular conditions.
