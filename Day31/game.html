🛰️ AI Supply Chain Control Tower

You are the Head of Operations. For the next 3 minutes, live disruptions will hit your global network — ports, factories, trucks, and warehouses. Decide fast, decide well.

  • Alerts appear in real time with a priority level and countdown.
  • Pick an action before the timer runs out — the right call improves KPIs, the wrong one hurts them.
  • Some consequences are delayed — a decision may echo a few seconds later.
  • Ignoring or letting an alert expire always costs you.
  • Survive the full session and see your final grade.

❔ How To Play

Quick reference for running the control tower.

  • Watch the KPI strip at the top — Service Level, CSAT, Inventory Health, Transport Efficiency, Cost, Revenue Protected.
  • Each alert card shows a priority badge (Critical / High / Medium) and a shrinking time bar.
  • Choose an action button — each alert has one clearly best action, several plausible ones, and traps like "Ignore."
  • Alert frequency increases as the clock runs down — expect overlapping crises late in the shift.
  • Use Pause anytime to catch your breath — the timer and alerts freeze.
