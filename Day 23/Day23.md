PrepAI India – Customer & MVP Blueprint

Overview

PrepAI India is an AI-powered Interview Preparation and ATS Resume Builder designed to help Indian job seekers receive personalized feedback, improve interview performance, and increase their chances of securing job offers.

Problem Statement

Most job seekers receive rejection emails without understanding what went wrong. Existing preparation resources are generic and fail to provide personalized feedback.

Key problems:

- No feedback after interview rejection
- ATS resume filtering issues
- Lack of confidence in HR and behavioral rounds
- Expensive coaching alternatives
- No structured improvement mechanism

Customer Segments

Freshers

- Age: 20–24
- Final-year B.Tech/MBA students
- Seeking first job opportunities
- Budget-conscious users

Career Switchers

- Age: 26–34
- 3–7 years of experience
- Looking for higher-paying roles
- Willing to pay for faster results

Core Value Proposition

PrepAI acts as a personalized interview coach by providing:

- AI mock interviews
- Resume ATS analysis
- Personalized improvement reports
- Role-specific recommendations

Top Customer Pain Points

1. No personalized feedback after interview failure
2. Resume rejected by ATS systems
3. Difficulty communicating achievements
4. Generic interview preparation content
5. No realistic interview practice environment
6. Salary negotiation challenges
7. Fear of behavioral rounds
8. Weak LinkedIn profiles
9. Expensive coaching programs
10. Gap between preparation and actual performance

Key Risks

1. AI feedback quality inconsistency
2. User drop-off after first session
3. Low willingness to pay
4. Competition from larger platforms
5. Founder bandwidth constraints

Success Metrics

- 100 Week-1 Users
- 20 Paying Customers
- 40%+ Retention
- NPS > 45
- 60%+ Session Completion Rate

30-Day Execution Plan

Validation

- Conduct additional interviews
- Complete Wizard-of-Oz testing
- Finalize Go/No-Go decision

Build

- Create AI mock interview engine
- Develop ATS resume scoring
- Launch onboarding flow

Beta

- Onboard first 20 users
- Collect feedback
- Improve retention metrics

Key Learning

Users do not need more content.

They need personalized feedback, measurable improvement, and confidence before interviews.

Customer-centric product development significantly increases the likelihood of achieving product-market fit.
