The problem: it's a tag cloud, not a headline. Eight keywords separated by pipes with no hierarchy — recruiters can't tell what you actually specialize in vs. what you've touched once.

Invisible cost: you DO show up in keyword search (that part works), but you lose the click-through — the headline is the ad copy, and this ad copy sells nothing.
